WP3 · HEAD TRACKER SETUP
Before we start
Follow these steps to ensure accurate head pose estimation. This only takes a few seconds.
1
Grant camera access
Allow the browser to use your webcam when prompted. No video is transmitted — all processing runs locally.
2
Look directly at the camera
Position your face in the centre of the frame, approximately 50–80 cm from the screen. Keep your face fully visible — forehead to chin.
3
Ensure good lighting
Face a light source (window or lamp) so your face is evenly lit. Avoid strong backlight or harsh shadows across your face.
4
Hold still — calibrating
Once your face is detected, hold a neutral, forward-facing position for 2 seconds to set the baseline orientation.
